**Hao Li (李昊)** is a professor at the [School of Computer Science and Technology](https://www.cs.xjtu.edu.cn/),
[Xi’an Jiaotong University](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/) in P.R. China.
He received his Ph.D. degree from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Xi’an Jiaotong University in 2016.
His research interests include networked systems, programmable networks, middleboxes, and network verification.
He is a member of [ANTS Lab](https://ants.xjtu.edu.cn/).
