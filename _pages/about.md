I am a professor at the [School of Computer Science and Technology](https://www.cs.xjtu.edu.cn/),
[Xi’an Jiaotong University](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/), and a member of [ANTS Lab](https://ants.xjtu.edu.cn/).
I received my Ph.D. from XJTU in 2016.

My research focuses on **networked systems**, including programmable networks,
middleboxes, network simulation and emulation, and network verification.
