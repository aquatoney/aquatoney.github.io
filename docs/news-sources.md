Author details and other bibliography metadata have not been provided, so the announcement does not introduce an incomplete entry in `papers.bib`.

The 2026 IEEE Transactions on Computers article remains in Publications. No acceptance news is assigned because its actual journal acceptance date is not available.

CCF A/B/C labels retain the pre-migration data; these ranks have not been independently reassessed. The legacy `d` value is not rendered as “CCF D”, since it is not an official CCF rank.
